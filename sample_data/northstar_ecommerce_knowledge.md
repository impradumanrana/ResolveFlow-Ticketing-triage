## NSM-001 — Track a dispatched order

**Category:** account

Northstar Market customers can open **Order History**, select the order, and choose **Track shipment** after dispatch. Standard delivery is normally 3–5 business days and express delivery is normally 1–2 business days after dispatch. The first carrier scan may take up to 24 hours to appear. Ask for the order ID when the published window has passed. Route to Shipping Review when tracking has not changed for three business days, the carrier reports the parcel lost, or the delivery estimate has expired.

## NSM-002 — Delivery is late

**Category:** account

Confirm the dispatch date, shipping service, destination region, and latest carrier scan. Weekends, public holidays, severe weather, and regional disruption can extend estimates. If the parcel remains inside the published window, explain the estimate and ask the customer to monitor tracking. If it is beyond the window or has no movement for three business days, collect the order ID and tracking reference and route to Shipping Review. Do not promise a replacement, refund, or exact arrival date before the carrier investigation.

## NSM-003 — Marked delivered but not received

**Category:** account

Ask the customer to check the delivery address, safe places, reception, household members, and nearby neighbours. Carrier delivery scans can appear several hours early. If a courier says the box arrived but nobody can find it, wait until 24 hours after the scan, collect the order ID, and confirm the address through the approved secure workflow. Route to Shipping Review for a carrier investigation. Do not promise an immediate refund or replacement and do not request unnecessary identity or payment information.

## NSM-004 — Cancel an order

**Category:** account

An order can be cancelled only before warehouse packing begins. The customer should open **Order History** and choose **Cancel order** if the control is visible. If the control is absent, collect the order ID and route to Order Support. Do not promise cancellation once packing or dispatch has started. After delivery, an eligible product can follow the normal return workflow. A payment authorization may remain pending temporarily after a successful cancellation and is not evidence of a completed charge.

## NSM-005 — Change a delivery address

**Category:** account

The delivery address can be changed only before warehouse packing begins. Ask for the order ID and direct the customer to the approved secure address-change workflow; do not collect a complete address in ordinary chat. If packing or dispatch has started, explain that Northstar Market cannot edit the label. Route urgent shipped-order cases to Order Support to check carrier options, without promising redirection. Suspected account changes made by someone else must go to Security Ops.

## NSM-006 — Standard return policy

**Category:** billing

Most unused products in original condition can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. Final-sale products, personalized goods, perishable products, and opened hygiene items are normally excluded. The customer should start the return from **Order History** and follow the supplied label instructions. Ask for the order ID and item name if eligibility is unclear. Do not promise approval before checking the exclusions, and route damaged, unsafe, or incorrectly supplied products through their dedicated workflows.

## NSM-007 — Exchange size or colour

**Category:** billing

Eligible unused apparel and footwear can be exchanged during the 30-day return period while replacement stock is available. The customer should open **Order History** and choose **Exchange**. If the desired option is unavailable, the customer can return the eligible item and place a new order. Final-sale and personalized products are normally excluded. Escalate policy exceptions or inventory discrepancies rather than promising stock, reserving an item, or manually changing the original order.

## NSM-008 — Refund processing time

**Category:** billing

After a return is received and approved, Northstar Market sends the refund to the original payment method. Most banks display it within 5–10 business days after the refund confirmation. Ask for the order ID and confirmation date. If ten business days have passed, route to Billing Review. Never request a full card number or security code. Do not claim that a bank has completed processing without evidence, and do not promise an alternative payment method.

## NSM-009 — Damaged product

**Category:** account

Collect the order ID, affected item, packaging condition, and clear photos when available. Ask the customer to keep the product and packaging until review is complete. Route leaking batteries, smoke, overheating, sharp exposed parts, contamination, or injury concerns to urgent human review. For ordinary damage, Order Support can decide whether policy supports a replacement or refund. Do not ask the customer to operate an unsafe product and do not promise an outcome before evidence review.

## NSM-010 — Wrong or missing item

**Category:** account

Collect the order ID, the item received, the expected item, and photos of the product and packing slip when available. For a missing item, first confirm whether Order History shows a split shipment. Route the evidence to Order Support. A verified fulfilment error should not require the customer to pay return shipping, but support must not promise a refund or replacement before review. Suspected package tampering or repeated high-value claims require human investigation.

## NSM-011 — Duplicate charge or payment authorization

**Category:** billing

First distinguish a pending authorization from two completed charges. Ask for the order ID, transaction dates, amounts, and only approved payment identifiers such as the last four digits through the secure workflow. Never request a full card number or security code. Two completed charges, chargebacks, suspected fraud, or an angry financial dispute must go to Billing Review and cannot be auto-resolved. A single pending authorization may expire according to the customer’s bank timeline.

## NSM-012 — Card declined at checkout

**Category:** billing

Ask the customer to verify the billing address, card expiry, available funds, and bank approval, or try another supported payment method. Northstar Market cannot see or override the bank’s private decline reason. Never request the full card number, security code, password, or one-time code. Repeated failures, an account restriction, suspected fraud, or urgent financial impact must be routed to Billing Review. Do not advise repeated rapid retries because they can create additional authorizations.

## NSM-013 — Download or correct an invoice

**Category:** billing

After payment is captured, customers can open **Order History**, select the order, and choose **Download invoice**. A copy is also sent to the purchase email. If the invoice is missing, ask for the order ID or purchase email through the approved workflow. Legal-name, tax-ID, or billing-address changes to an issued invoice require Billing Review and verification. Do not alter historical invoice details in chat or expose an invoice belonging to another account.

## NSM-014 — Update a saved payment method

**Category:** billing

Customers can add or remove a saved payment method under **Account Settings → Payments**. The payment method for an already placed order normally cannot be changed. For subscriptions, update the default method before the next renewal date. Never collect full card data in support chat. An unrecognized saved card, unexpected payment change, or change made after a suspicious login must be routed to Security Ops rather than treated as routine billing maintenance.

## NSM-015 — Gift-card help

**Category:** billing

Gift cards can be entered at checkout and do not expire. They are not normally redeemable for cash except where law requires. Ask for the purchase reference, but never request the complete gift-card code in ordinary chat. A lost, stolen, blocked, or unexpectedly empty card requires Billing Review and proof of purchase. Do not reveal a gift-card balance to an unverified requester and do not issue or replace value without authorization.

## NSM-016 — Promotional codes

**Category:** billing

One valid promotional code can be entered at checkout before payment. Codes normally cannot be combined or applied after an order is placed. Ask the customer to check expiry, product exclusions, minimum spend, geography, and account eligibility. If the displayed terms were met but the code still fails, collect a screenshot and route for review. Do not create a code, promise a discount, or manually adjust a completed order without authorization.

## NSM-017 — Cancel a product subscription

**Category:** billing

The customer can open **Account Settings → Subscriptions** and choose **Cancel** before the next renewal date. Cancellation stops future renewals but does not automatically refund a completed charge. If the cancellation control is missing, the subscription still renews, or a disputed charge has completed, collect the subscription reference and route to Billing Review. Do not promise retroactive cancellation or a refund; refund eligibility must be reviewed under the applicable policy.

## NSM-018 — Backorders and preorders

**Category:** account

Backordered products ship when inventory is replenished; preorder products ship on or near the current estimated release date. Estimates can change and are not guarantees. Customers can review the latest estimate in **Order History**. If cancellation remains available, they may cancel before packing. Route a materially missed estimate, absent update, or unavailable cancellation control to Order Support. Do not invent a restock date, reserve stock, or promise dispatch.

## NSM-019 — International duties and taxes

**Category:** billing

International orders may incur import duties, taxes, or carrier handling fees imposed by local authorities. These charges are separate from Northstar Market prices unless checkout explicitly states otherwise. Support cannot waive government charges. Route an amount charged directly by Northstar Market that was not disclosed at checkout to Billing Review. For a government assessment, explain that the customer should contact local customs. Do not provide legal or tax advice.

## NSM-020 — Marketplace seller orders

**Category:** account

Marketplace products identify the seller on the product page and order record. Customers should contact the seller through the secure order message thread. Northstar Market can intervene when the seller misses the published response window, provides invalid tracking, or violates marketplace policy. Keep communication on-platform. Route suspected counterfeit, fraudulent, prohibited, or unsafe listings to urgent marketplace review and do not ask the customer to contact the seller outside the platform.

## NSM-021 — Password reset

**Category:** technical

Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page and follow the time-limited link sent to the account email. Check spam folders and request a fresh link if the previous one expired. Support must never ask for or disclose a password or one-time code. If the customer no longer controls the account email, route to Account Recovery for identity verification. Unexpected reset messages or account changes the customer did not request require Security Ops review.

## NSM-022 — Two-factor authentication

**Category:** technical

Enable two-factor authentication under **Account Settings → Security** using a supported authenticator app, then store recovery codes securely. If codes fail, check that the device time is automatic and try a saved recovery code. Support must never request a current one-time code. A lost device requires identity verification through Account Recovery. Unknown prompts, suspicious logins, or changed security settings require immediate Security Ops review.

## NSM-023 — Locked account recovery

**Category:** account

Repeated failed sign-ins can temporarily lock an account. The customer should wait for the period shown on screen and then use the recovery flow. If access remains blocked, collect the account identifier through the secure workflow and route to Account Recovery. Do not bypass identity verification or reveal whether another person owns an account. A lockout accompanied by unknown activity, changed details, or unrecognized orders is a security incident.

## NSM-024 — Suspected account takeover

**Category:** account

Unknown logins—including a sign-in from overseas—changed account details, unrecognized purchases, or password-reset messages not requested by the customer are critical security concerns. Advise the customer to reset the password from a trusted device if safe, secure the associated email account, and avoid approving unexpected authentication prompts. Immediately route to Security Ops. Never auto-resolve, reveal internal risk controls, request passwords or one-time codes, or promise that the account is secure.

## NSM-025 — Privacy access, export, and deletion

**Category:** account

Privacy access, correction, export, and deletion requests must use the verified **Privacy Request** workflow in Account Settings. Legal retention requirements may apply to transaction records. Do not ask for government identification in ordinary chat or disclose data before verification. Route identity-verification problems, legal deadlines, requests involving another person’s data, or complaints about unlawful processing to the Privacy Team. Support cannot guarantee immediate deletion.

## NSM-026 — Mobile app crashes or freezes

**Category:** technical

Ask for the device type, operating-system version, Northstar app version, screen where the failure occurs, and whether the issue persists after restarting. Recommend installing the latest supported app version and retrying on a stable network. Do not ask the customer to disable device security or share passwords. If checkout or account access remains blocked, route to Tier-1 Technical with the diagnostic details. Data loss, repeated payment attempts, or security warnings require human review.

## NSM-027 — Order confirmation email is missing

**Category:** account

Ask the customer to check spam, promotions, and the email used at checkout. A signed-in customer can also review **Order History**. If the order appears there, no duplicate purchase is needed. If no record appears, collect the approximate purchase time and amount through the secure workflow and route to Order Support. Do not expose orders associated with another email and do not advise placing the order again until payment status is checked.

## NSM-028 — Product warranty claim

**Category:** account

Warranty eligibility depends on the product, purchase date, seller, and failure type. When headphones or another product fail within the warranty period, collect the order ID, product serial or model, purchase age, fault description, and photos or video when safe. Accidental damage and normal wear may be excluded. Route the evidence to Warranty Review; do not promise repair, replacement, or refund before eligibility is confirmed. Overheating, smoke, electric shock, or injury concerns require urgent safety escalation.

## NSM-029 — Loyalty points are missing

**Category:** billing

Eligible loyalty points normally appear after an order is paid and the return window has closed. Cancelled, refunded, excluded, or guest purchases may not earn points. Ask for the order ID and loyalty account identifier through the secure workflow. If the eligibility window has passed and points are still absent, route to Billing Review. Do not manually credit points, merge accounts, or disclose another customer’s balance without verification.

## NSM-030 — Business and bulk orders

**Category:** account

For a formal quote or bulk purchase—including hundreds of units such as two hundred monitors—collect the product names, quantities, destination country, required delivery date, and business invoicing needs without collecting payment credentials. Route requests above normal storefront quantity limits to Business Sales. Standard stock and delivery estimates are not commitments for bulk orders. Tender terms, negotiated pricing, tax exemptions, resale certificates, or contract changes require human review and must not be auto-approved.
