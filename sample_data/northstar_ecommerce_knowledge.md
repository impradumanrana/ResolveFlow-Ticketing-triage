# Northstar Market Support Knowledge

Synthetic demonstration content for ResolveFlow. Version 1.0 · 31 August 2026.

## Track a dispatched order

Customers can open Order History and select Track shipment after a parcel is dispatched. Standard delivery normally takes 3–5 business days after dispatch and express delivery normally takes 1–2 business days. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first carrier scan. Ask for the order ID if the expected window has passed. Escalate when tracking has not changed for three business days or the carrier reports the parcel lost.

## Delivery is late

Confirm the dispatch date, shipping service, destination region, and latest carrier scan. Weekends, public holidays, weather, and regional disruptions can extend estimates. Ask the customer to allow the published delivery window before opening an investigation. If the parcel is beyond that window or tracking has been inactive for three business days, send it to Shipping Review with the order ID and tracking reference.

## Marked delivered but not received

Ask the customer to check the delivery address, safe places, reception, household members, and nearby neighbours. Carrier scans can occasionally appear several hours early. If the parcel is still missing after 24 hours, collect the order ID and confirm the delivery address without requesting unnecessary personal data. Escalate for a carrier investigation; do not promise an immediate refund or replacement.

## Cancel an order

An order can be cancelled only before warehouse packing begins. The customer should open Order History and choose Cancel order if the option is available. If the option is absent, collect the order ID and route the request to Order Support. Do not promise cancellation after dispatch. The customer may use the normal return process after delivery if the product is eligible.

## Change the delivery address

Delivery addresses can be changed only before warehouse packing begins. Ask for the order ID and the corrected address through the approved secure workflow. Never request payment credentials. If packing or dispatch has started, explain that the address cannot be edited and route urgent cases to Order Support to check carrier options.

## Standard return policy

Most unused products in original condition may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. Final-sale products, personalized goods, and opened hygiene products are normally excluded. The customer should start a return from Order History and follow the supplied label instructions. Ask for the order ID and item name when eligibility is unclear. Do not promise approval before checking exclusions.

## Exchange size or colour

Eligible unused apparel and footwear may be exchanged within the 30-day return period while replacement stock is available. Start from Order History and choose Exchange. If the desired option is unavailable, use a return and place a new order. Final-sale and personalized products are not normally exchangeable. Escalate exceptions rather than promising stock.

## Refund processing time

After a return is received and approved, Northstar Market sends the refund to the original payment method. Banks normally display it within 5–10 business days. Ask for the order ID and refund-confirmation date. If ten business days have passed, route the case to Billing Review. Never ask for a full card number and do not claim the bank has completed processing without evidence.

## Damaged product

Ask for the order ID, affected item, packaging condition, and clear photos of the damage. Advise the customer to keep the product and packaging until review is complete. Route safety-related damage, leaking batteries, smoke, overheating, or injury concerns to urgent human review. For ordinary damage, Order Support can approve a replacement or refund under policy.

## Wrong or missing item

Collect the order ID, the item received, the expected item, and photos of the product and packing slip when available. For a missing item, confirm whether the shipment was split into multiple parcels. Route the evidence to Order Support. Do not ask the customer to pay return shipping for a verified fulfilment error and do not promise an outcome before review.

## Duplicate charge

First distinguish a temporary authorization from two completed charges. Ask for the order ID, transaction dates, amounts, and the last four digits only through the approved secure workflow. Never request a full card number. Completed duplicate charges, chargebacks, fraud claims, or an angry financial dispute require Billing Review and must not be automatically resolved.

## Card declined

Ask the customer to verify the billing address, card expiry, available funds, and bank approval or try another payment method. Northstar Market cannot see the bank’s private decline reason. Never ask for the full card number or security code. Repeated failures, suspected fraud, or urgent financial impact must be routed to Billing Review.

## Download an invoice

Customers can open Order History, select an order, and choose Download invoice after payment is captured. An invoice is also sent to the purchase email. If it is missing, ask for the order ID or purchase email and route business tax-detail changes to Billing Review. Do not alter historical invoice details without verification.

## Update a saved payment method

Customers can add or remove a saved payment method under Account Settings → Payments. A payment method for an already placed order normally cannot be changed. Never collect full card data in support chat. For subscription renewals, update the default method before the next billing date. Escalate unauthorized payment-method changes.

## Gift cards

Gift cards can be entered at checkout and do not expire. They are not normally redeemable for cash except where law requires. Ask for the gift-card reference but never request the complete code in ordinary chat. Lost, stolen, blocked, or unexpectedly empty cards require Billing Review and proof of purchase.

## Promotional codes

Enter one valid promotional code at checkout before payment. Codes normally cannot be combined or applied after an order is placed. Check expiry, product exclusions, minimum spend, and account eligibility. If the displayed terms were met but the code fails, collect a screenshot and route it for review. Do not manually promise a discount.

## Cancel a product subscription

Open Account Settings → Subscriptions and choose Cancel before the next renewal date. Cancellation stops future renewals but does not automatically refund a completed charge. If the cancellation control is missing or a charge has already completed, collect the subscription reference and route it to Billing Review. Refund requests remain subject to policy.

## Backorders and preorders

Backordered products ship when replenished; preorder products ship on or near the estimated release date. Estimates can change and are not guarantees. Customers can review the latest date in Order History. If cancellation remains available, they may cancel before packing. Route a materially missed estimate or unavailable cancellation to Order Support.

## International duties and taxes

International orders may incur import duties, taxes, or carrier handling fees imposed by local authorities. These charges are separate from Northstar Market’s product and shipping prices unless checkout explicitly states otherwise. Support cannot waive government charges. Route disputes about an amount charged by Northstar Market to Billing Review and advise customers to contact local customs for government assessments.

## Marketplace seller orders

Marketplace products show the seller on the product page and order record. Customers should contact the seller through the secure order message thread. Northstar Market can intervene when the seller misses the response window, tracking is invalid, or the item violates marketplace policy. Keep all communication on-platform and escalate suspected counterfeit, unsafe, or fraudulent listings.

## Password reset

Use Forgot password on the sign-in page and follow the time-limited link sent to the account email. Check spam folders and request a new link if the old one expired. Never share a password or one-time code with support. Unexpected reset messages or changes the customer did not request require Security Ops review.

## Two-factor authentication

Enable two-factor authentication in Account Settings → Security using an authenticator app. Store recovery codes securely. If codes fail, check device time and try a recovery code. Support must never ask for a current one-time code. Lost-device cases require identity verification; suspicious login activity requires Security Ops.

## Locked account recovery

Repeated failed sign-ins can temporarily lock an account. Wait for the displayed period and then use the recovery flow. If access is still blocked, collect the account identifier through the secure workflow and route to Account Recovery. Do not bypass identity verification. A lockout accompanied by unknown activity or changed details is a security incident.

## Suspected account takeover

Unknown logins, changed account details, unrecognized orders, or password-reset messages not requested by the customer are critical security concerns. Advise the customer to reset the password from a trusted device if safe and secure the associated email account. Immediately route to Security Ops. Never auto-resolve, reveal internal data, or request passwords or one-time codes.

## Privacy and account deletion

Privacy access, correction, export, and deletion requests must use the verified Privacy Request workflow in Account Settings. Explain that legal retention requirements may apply to transaction records. Do not ask for government ID in ordinary chat. Route identity-verification problems, legal deadlines, or requests involving another person’s data to the Privacy Team.
